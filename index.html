Introduction to Electrocardiography

An electrocardiogram (ECG or EKG) is a non-invasive test that records the electrical activity of the heart over time.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand what an ECG measures.

The ECG records electrical impulses generated by cardiac depolarization and repolarization. It does not directly measure the mechanical contraction of the heart.

Clinical Uses

  • Detection of cardiac arrhythmias.

Cardiac Anatomy and Electrical Conduction

The Cardiac Conduction System
